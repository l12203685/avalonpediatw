- [major] The pre-fixed bins miss a strong public R1 signal that only exists when a thumb captains before Percival (C1/C2): one thumb putting the other thumb on its own talk team. Because of this, the claim that the only usable thumb information is the configuration is wrong (summary row 'thumb position', section 3.4). The 54%/64% R1 Merlin-pick rates described as 'a little information' come almost entirely from this signal. The blanket R1 no-thumbs rule goes against the human majority in exactly these cases. These cases are not 'no information', so the rule stretches the owner's default ('when there is no information, don't send') past its stated scope.
  evidence: Independent Python parse of sheets_cache.json, same 1970 games. R1 talk proposals: a Morgana captain puts Merlin on her team 153/750 (20.4%); a Merlin captain puts Morgana on hers 21/773 (2.7%). So when one thumb includes the other, the included thumb is Merlin 87.9% [82.3, 92.0] 153/174. On Percival's R1 proposals that come after an earlier thumb included the other thumb (46 of 941): humans put the included thumb on their team 34/46 = 73.9% [59.7, 84.4] (talk 21/28 = 75.0% [56.6, 87.3], forced 13/18), and it is Merlin 39/46. Without these cases, R1 talk single-thumb picks are Merlin only 32/73 = 43.8% (chance); with them, 19/21. In C1 R1 talk, when the earlier thumb's team held the later thumb, Percival includes the later thumb 19/25 (76%), against 14/132 when it did not.
